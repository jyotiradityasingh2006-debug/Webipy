Our Process

1. Discovery

We talk. You tell us about your business, your customers, and what's missing online.

2. Design

We send a mockup. You say what you like and what you don't — no design-speak needed.

3. Development

We build it by hand, test it on phones, and fix the fiddly bits before you see it.

4. Launch

We put it online, connect your domain, and stick around for 30 days to smooth out anything odd.
